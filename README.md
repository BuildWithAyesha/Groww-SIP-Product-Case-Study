## 📌 Overview

Together We Groww is a product case study focused on improving SIP conversion among first-time investors on Groww.
While Groww makes mutual fund investing accessible with low entry points and a digital-first experience, first-time investors can still hesitate to start a SIP due to decision fatigue, financial anxiety, complex information, and friction during onboarding.

Our team explored the user journey from fund discovery to SIP activation, identified the highest-friction moments, and developed a set of product interventions designed to make the first SIP journey simpler, more personalized, and confidence-building.

## 🎯 Core Opportunity
How might we help first-time investors move from exploring SIPs to confidently starting their first SIP?

### 💡 Vision
Help first-time investors begin their SIP journey with clarity, confidence, and zero overwhelm—while building long-term trust and investing habits on Groww.

### 🎯 Mission
Empower first-time investors to build long-term wealth by making SIP investing on Groww simple, personalized, transparent, and emotionally reassuring.

## 🔎 The Problem
Our research indicated that first-time investors often explore mutual funds but hesitate to commit.

### Key friction points included:
1) Decision fatigue from too many funds, metrics, and choices
2) Fear of losing money and uncertainty around investment decisions
3) KYC and onboarding friction
4) Lack of personalized, goal-based guidance
5) Complex financial terminology and fund metrics
6) Uncertainty about which fund is appropriate for a beginner
7) Limited clarity around Groww's value compared with traditional channels

### Key research findings
1) 45% of surveyed users were unsure which fund to invest in
2) 64% wanted a beginner-friendly tag for easy-to-start mutual funds
3) 59% wanted easier-to-understand fund recommendations

These findings helped us frame the problem around confidence and decision-making, rather than simply adding more investment information.

## 👤 Target User
We developed three user personas:

### a) The Rani — Aspiring & Anxious
An early-career professional who wants to start investing but lacks confidence and financial knowledge.
#### Key needs:
Simple language · Guidance · Trust signals · Low-risk starting experience

### b) The Pia — Engaged & Indecisive
A research-oriented investor who understands the basics but struggles to choose between similar funds.
#### Key needs:
Clear trade-offs · Personalized recommendations · Decision confidence

### c) The Kabir Khan — Established & Uncommitted
An experienced investor already using multiple platforms and looking for stronger reasons to move or consolidate investments.
#### Key needs:
Advanced insights · Tax tools · Portfolio intelligence · Differentiated value

## 🎯 Primary Persona: Rani

We prioritized Rani because she represents an important conversion opportunity: moving a user from zero SIPs to their first SIP.
The product opportunity was therefore not to provide more information, but to reduce uncertainty at the moments when information becomes overwhelming.

## 🧭 User Journey
We mapped the end-to-end SIP journey:
#### Awareness → Exploration → Evaluation → Onboarding → Commitment → Investment → Retention

The most important friction moments appeared around:
1) Fund discovery
2) Fund comparison
3) Risk profiling
4) KYC completion
5) SIP amount and frequency selection
6) First-investment hesitation
7) Post-investment anxiety

This helped us prioritize interventions across both conversion and confidence.

## 🚀 Proposed Product Solutions

### 1. GrowwMentor Picks
#### The problem
First-time investors can feel overwhelmed by the number of available funds and worry about making the wrong choice.
#### The solution
A curated Mentor Picks experience presenting beginner-friendly SIP options based on risk level and relevant user context.
- Expected experience impact
- Reduces decision fatigue
- Builds confidence
- Makes fund discovery easier
- Business impact
- Faster decision-making
- Higher SIP initiation
- Lower drop-off during fund selection

### 2. Agentic AI KYC Video Flow
#### The problem
KYC can feel lengthy, technical, and impersonal—particularly for first-time investors.
#### The solution
An AI-guided video experience that walks users through the KYC process step by step.
- Expected experience impact
- More reassuring onboarding
- Reduced perceived complexity
- Greater guidance during KYC
- Business impact
- Improved KYC completion
- More users progressing toward SIP activation

### 3. Fund Discovery Compass
#### The problem
Users encounter numerous categories, metrics, and financial terms while searching for a suitable fund.
#### The solution
A visual, goal- and preference-based discovery experience that helps users narrow down relevant funds without requiring deep financial expertise.
- Expected experience impact
- Simplifies exploration
- Reduces cognitive load
- Helps users reach a decision faster
- Business impact
- Higher meaningful engagement
- More fund shortlisting
- Increased SIP initiation

### 4. SIP Trial Mode — ₹5,000 Simulator
#### The problem
First-time investors may hesitate because they are uncertain about what investing through an SIP could look like over time.
#### The solution
A simulation experience allowing users to explore how a hypothetical ₹5,000 SIP could have performed historically.
- Expected experience impact
- Makes SIP investing more tangible
- Helps users understand potential outcomes
- Reduces uncertainty before committing
- Business impact
- Increased confidence
- Higher transition from exploration to SIP initiation

### Note: Any simulation should clearly communicate that historical performance does not guarantee future returns.

## 💡 Additional Concepts
We also explored several supporting opportunities:
#### 1) Smart Guardrails
User-defined rules designed to help investors manage their experience during market volatility.

#### 2) SIP Personality Quiz → Smart Feed
A lightweight onboarding experience that maps user preferences and risk appetite to a personalized SIP discovery feed.

#### 3) Invest With Friends
A social investing concept allowing users to start SIPs alongside friends while maintaining individual investments.

#### 4) SIP Summary Card
A simplified fund view highlighting three key metrics to reduce information overload.

#### 5) Clickable Jargon Explainers
Contextual explanations for financial terms directly within the investment journey.

## ⚠️ Risks & Mitigations
1) RiskMitigation
2) Curated picks may reduce user control
3) Clearly distinguish recommendations from the full fund universe
4) AI KYC may feel unnecessary or gimmicky
5) Keep it optional and retain a conventional KYC path
6) Discovery Compass may oversimplify investing
7) Provide an advanced/detailed view
8) Simulator may create unrealistic expectations
9) Use clear disclaimers and show multiple historical scenarios

## 🧠 Product Thinking
A key insight from the project was:
1) First-time investors don't necessarily need more information—they need better decision support.
2) Our approach therefore focused on transforming a potentially overwhelming investment journey into one that provides:
Context → Guidance → Confidence → Action
3) Rather than replacing user choice, the proposed experience aims to assist users in making informed decisions while keeping them in control.
